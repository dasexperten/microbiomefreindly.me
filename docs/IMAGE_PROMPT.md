# The image prompt — one paragraph, filled per article

**As of:** 2026-09-03 · **Owner decision:** images are regenerated from the article's own text as striking 3D scientific infographics, not staged portraits. The prompt is written by **Roberta with Magnus** (Magnus supplies the mechanism, the morphology and the numbers; Roberta shapes the words), generated by **Lisa** in **Grok or Codex** — **not Higgsfield for this lane** — and checked by **Marika** against `docs/BRAND_IMAGE_SPEC.md` before it reaches a page.

Fill the braces from the article's front matter and body. Nothing else changes.

---

## The paragraph

> A striking, physically-lit 3D scientific infographic that explains **{the mechanism in one clause, from the article's answer paragraph}** for a curious reader who is not a scientist: the frame is built around **{the organism or structure, with its real morphology in plain words — rod, coccus, forked Y-shaped cell, spore at one end, spiral, filament, biofilm sheet, mucus layer, villi}**, rendered as clean matte 3D volumes with soft subsurface translucency, shallow depth of field and one warm directional light from the upper left, arranged so the eye reads the process in one path — **{what happens first} → {what happens next} → {the outcome}** — with the single most important number of the piece, **{the number and its unit, exactly as sourced}**, expressed *visually* through scale, quantity or proportion rather than written anywhere; the ground is the portal's warm greige `#EFEBE7` to paper `#F7F5F2`, the forms are charcoal `#363636`, deep navy `#1F2A3E` and teal `#1B5F56`, with at most one accent — gold `#C7A24B`, coral `#E2725B` or lime `#D9EB99` — placed only on the element the reader must notice; the composition is a **{3:2 preview, subject filling 55–70 % of the frame, centre band safe for a 1.91:1 crop | 16:9 hero, subject in the left or right 45 %, the rest calm negative space}** born natively at that ratio, never cropped from another; absolutely no text, letters, numerals, labels, arrows with words, scale bars, watermarks, invented microscope or screen interfaces, no product, packaging, capsule, bottle or brand mark of any kind, no people, faces, hands or bodies, no medical horror, no black background, no blue neon glow, no false-colour electron-micrograph cliché and no stock DNA helix; the mood is calm laboratory daylight and quiet authority — an illustration of a micro-world that is honest about being an illustration, in the visual language of a modern science magazine cover; output at **{2048×1365 for 3:2 | 2400×1350 for 16:9}** or larger, sharp, matte, no vignette, no border, no frame.

## Rules that outlive any engine

1. Text in an image is forbidden — the caption and alt text carry the words.
2. A number is shown by quantity, scale or proportion, never printed, and only if it is sourced.
3. Morphology is truth: a *Lactobacillus* article never gets spheres.
4. Each frame is its own world; ten cards must not read as ten crops of one plate.
5. No product, no faces, no invented packaging — nothing that could be mistaken for a real photograph of a real thing.
6. Marika accepts the frame and then the frame on the live page. Two looks, not one.

## The rest of what Lisa needs

**The single idea both images must carry.** One appetite, two outcomes: this organism eats the coat that protects the gut wall, and whether that reads as renewal or as erosion depends on what else there is to eat. The preview shows the appetite next to the wall. The hero shows a person who will say both halves of that out loud.

**Colour, from the brand palette.** Preview: ivory ground, the mucus gel in ivory and the palest green, the rods in navy and teal, one small gold note where the light catches a strand of the gel. One accent at most, as a note, never a wash. Hero: the same warm ground, skin true to the REF, navy and green in the room, one accent.

**Scale cue for the preview.** The folded lining underneath, going soft and unreadable with distance — so the eye understands it is standing very close to something small, without any bar, number or label saying so.

**File-name keyword.** `verrucomicrobiota` → `verrucomicrobiota-preview` · `verrucomicrobiota-hero` (Jurgen's pattern).

**Priority.** Evergreen — this is an encyclopedia bacteria entry, not news. No week deadline; correctness of the shape in field 4 matters more than speed.

**The mechanism in one sentence, for understanding.** *Akkermansia muciniphila* lives in the mucus layer of the colon and feeds on the mucins that make it, releasing short-chain fatty acids the lining burns as fuel and prompting the host to secrete more mucus — turnover, not net loss, at a moderate population; but in a fibre-starved or already inflamed gut the same feeding thins the barrier that keeps everything else off the wall.

**One honesty note.** The preview is an illustration of a micro-world. It is never captioned or presented as a real micrograph or a diagnostic image, in any locale.

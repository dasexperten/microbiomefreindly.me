## The rest of what Lisa and Otto need

**The single idea both images must carry.** One appetite, two outcomes: this organism eats the coat that protects the gut wall, and whether that reads as renewal or as erosion depends on what else there is to eat. The card frame shows the appetite next to the wall; the built frame walks from renewal to thinning.

**Colour, from the brand palette.** Ground warm greige to paper, the mucus gel in pale translucent paper tones, the rods in navy and teal, charcoal for shadow. One accent per frame, as Marika's block names it — a note, never a wash.

**Scale cue for the card frame.** The folded lining underneath, going soft and unreadable with distance — so the eye understands it is standing very close to something small, without any bar, number or label saying so.

**Priority.** Evergreen — this is an encyclopedia bacteria entry, not news. No week deadline; correctness of the shape in field 4 matters more than speed.

**The mechanism in one sentence, for understanding.** *Akkermansia muciniphila* lives in the mucus layer of the colon and feeds on the mucins that make it, releasing short-chain fatty acids the lining burns as fuel and prompting the host to secrete more mucus — turnover, not net loss, at a moderate population; but in a fibre-starved or already inflamed gut the same feeding thins the barrier that keeps everything else off the wall.

**One honesty note.** Both frames are illustrations of a micro-world. Neither is ever captioned or presented as a real micrograph or a diagnostic image, in any locale.

**File-name keyword.** `verrucomicrobiota` → `verrucomicrobiota-preview` · `verrucomicrobiota-hero` (Jurgen's pattern).

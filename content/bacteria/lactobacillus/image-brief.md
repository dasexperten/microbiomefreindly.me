## For Lisa — the idea and the mechanism

**The single idea both images carry:** *Lactobacillus* is real and useful, but it lives where it can sour its surroundings — a jar, a cavity, the vagina — and only barely in the gut everyone thinks it rules. The preview shows the organism doing its one trick in a sour film; the hero shows the scientist holding the jar and looking at the plate, the gap between "in the yogurt" and "in you".

**The mechanism in one sentence:** Lactobacilli ferment sugar into lactic acid, and that acid lowers the pH of any stagnant, sugar-rich, low-oxygen pocket they settle in — which protects the vagina and preserves fermented food, deepens a cavity once it exists, and is why they cannot hold much ground in the gut.

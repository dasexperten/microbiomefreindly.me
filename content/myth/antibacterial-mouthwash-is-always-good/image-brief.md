**2. The mechanism in one clause (from the `answer` field)**

A broad antiseptic rinse does not perform triage — it cannot sort the harmful microbes from the useful ones, so it flattens the resident community on the tongue and shuts down the nitrite step the body needs in order to relax blood vessels.

**3. The organism or structure, with its real morphology in plain words**

The subject is **not one species**. It is the resident mat of bacteria living on the back surface of the tongue, and the whole point of the article is that it is a mixed community — so the frame must show **several different cell shapes living together**, never one repeated shape:

- **The ground is the tongue's own surface**, seen close: a dense field of small finger-like and blunt cone-like papillae with deep, shaded grooves between them. That crypted, velvety landscape is where the mat lives — down in the grooves, not on a flat plate and not on bare glass.
- **The mat itself is a biofilm sheet**, a soft translucent film binding the cells together over the papillae, not loose cells floating in space.
- **Four shapes, mixed, in one mat** — this is what the article's own measured shift in the community looks like:
  - **short plump rods** with rounded ends, singles and pairs;
  - **small round cells in tight clusters**, like little bunched heaps;
  - **paired round cells**, two stuck together at a flat face;
  - **long slender spindles**, thin filaments tapering to a point at both ends.
- **Density is the story.** In the healthy state the grooves are packed and every one of the four shapes is present. There are no spores, no tails, no stalks, no branching trees, and nothing spiral.

Never a single-species field, never one shape repeated across the frame, never a smooth flat surface standing in for the tongue: the mixture and the grooved landscape are the truth of this frame.

**4. The three-beat process — first, next, outcome**

Built on the article's own H2 skeleton. The eye must walk it in one path.

1. **First — the rinse arrives and does not choose.** A clear wash sweeping across the packed mat in the grooves. It reaches every kind of cell at once: the plump rods, the clustered round cells, the pairs and the slender spindles are all in its path, with nothing steering it toward one kind and away from another. No triage.
2. **Next — the community that survives is flattened and lopsided.** The same grooves, days later: the mat is thinner and the balance is visibly wrong — some shapes crowd back in numbers while others are nearly gone, so the mixture that was there at the start is not the mixture that remains. This is the disruption, not a clean sweep.
3. **Outcome — the lane from tongue to artery closes.** From the intact mat a broad flow of fine particles rises and travels away from the tongue toward a soft, open vessel wall; from the thinned mat that flow is a thread, and the vessel wall it reaches is visibly tighter and narrower. The eye reads it as *the rinse does not choose → the mat is thinned and skewed → the vessel loses what it was being sent*.

**5. The single sourced number, shown by scale or quantity**

**Ninety per cent: oral nitrite production cut to a tenth.** Seven days of chlorhexidine rinsing cut oral nitrite production by 90 % and plasma nitrite by 25 %, and systolic and diastolic blood pressure rose by 2–3.5 mmHg — Kapil V. et al., *Free Radical Biology & Medicine*, 2013 (`s2` in the article, doi 10.1016/j.freeradbiomed.2012.11.013, PMID 23183324).

Shown as **proportion, in the third beat**: the flow leaving the intact mat is a wide, dense band of particles; the flow leaving the thinned mat is unmistakably **one tenth of it** — a thin thread beside a broad stream: about forty particles rising from the intact mat, four from the thinned one. The gap must be obvious at a glance without counting. The number is never printed, never labelled, never written anywhere in the pixels; scale and quantity carry all of it.

**2. The mechanism in one clause (from the `answer` field)**

Dose matters only inside one strain and one outcome — doubling a dose helped in one trial, while a hundredfold larger dose of the same organism changed nothing in another, so a label number is a claim about quantity, not about evidence.

**3. The organism or structure, with its real morphology in plain words**

Every organism named in this article is a lactobacillus, and every lactobacillus is a **rod**. That is the truth of the frame and it cannot be traded for anything rounder:

- **Straight or very slightly curved rods with blunt, rounded ends**, clearly longer than they are wide — roughly three to five times as long as thick. *Lactobacillus acidophilus* CL1285, *Lactobacillus casei* LBC80R and *Lactobacillus rhamnosus* GG all look like this.
- **Singles, pairs and short chains** of two to five cells lying end to end, the way rods that have just divided sit. Not long ropes, not tangles.
- **A plain surface, no ornament.** No spores swelling one end, no tails, no stalks, no branches. These are non-spore-forming rods; a spore in this frame would be a factual error.
- **The ground they sit on** — a soft translucent mucus film over the gut lining, the lining beneath it rising in a rank of finger-shaped folds. The cells rest in that film, never on bare glass or in empty space.

Never spheres, never a forked Y, never a spiral, never a filament, never a biofilm sheet: none of those belong here.

**4. The three-beat process — first, next, outcome**

Built on the article's own H2 skeleton. The eye must walk it in one path.

1. **First — doubling did something.** A modest crowd of rods in the mucus, and beside it the same rods at twice the quantity. Under the larger crowd the gut lining is calm and even; under the smaller one it is visibly more disturbed. More really was better here.
2. **Next — a hundredfold did nothing.** A second pairing, this time a small cluster of rods against a crowd a hundred times its size. Both sit over lining in the identical calm state. The enormous difference in quantity buys no difference at all below it.
3. **Outcome — the strain and the illness set the dose, not the number.** The frame resolves on the fact that the two pairings behave in opposite ways although the cells look the same: quantity alone predicts nothing. Read it as *more helped → a hundred times more did not → the count is not the answer*.

**5. The single sourced number, shown by scale or quantity**

**A hundredfold: 10¹⁰ CFU against 10¹² CFU — and no difference between them.** In 559 Indian children hospitalised with acute watery diarrhoea, *Lactobacillus rhamnosus* GG at 10¹⁰ CFU and at 10¹² CFU twice daily both beat oral rehydration alone and showed no significant difference from each other — Basu S. et al., *Journal of Clinical Gastroenterology*, 2009 (`s3` in the article, doi 10.1097/MCG.0b013e31815a5780, PMID 18813028).

Shown as **proportion**: one small cluster of rods and, beside it, a crowd unmistakably a hundred times denser — the size gap has to be shocking at a glance — sitting over gut lining that is in exactly the same state under both. The number is never printed, never labelled, never written anywhere in the pixels. Scale and quantity carry all of it.

## 2. Article title (EN) + one-line summary

**Same capsule, opposite result: what decides if a probiotic works**

A 12-week trial gave 120 overweight adults a heat-killed *Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* or placebo. On average nothing happened. Only the people whose gut had the fewest kinds of bacteria at the start lost more weight. It is a lead, not a proof.

## 3. Organism / structure in the preview

*Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* (formerly *Lactobacillus plantarum*), strain LRCC5282 — but **heat-killed**, so dead cells, not a live culture. They arrive as a signal from outside into an existing gut community sitting on the mucus layer of the gut wall. The real subject of the picture is the **community it lands in**: a thin, sparse one with open room.

## 4. Morphology, in words (truth)

- *L. plantarum*: straight rods with rounded ends, roughly three to eight times longer than wide, single, in pairs, or in short chains of a few cells. No flagella, no movement — and these are dead, so they lie still. Slightly softened, rounded outlines are honest for heat-killed cells; no bursting, no fragments.
- The resident community around them: a **thinned-out** gut population — only a few kinds of bacteria, spaced apart, on a pale mucus film over the finger-like folds of the gut lining. A mix of shorter plump rods and a few small round cells is truthful for such a gut. What must be visible is **room**: patches of bare mucus between the residents.
- Scale cue: the rods are a few micrometres long; the folds of gut lining behind them are hundreds of times larger, so the lining reads as soft rolling ground, out of focus, not as a wall.

Not spheres for *L. plantarum*. Not a dense carpet — the whole point of the article is that a thinned community has open seats.

## For Lisa — the idea both images must carry, and why

**Single idea:** *A newcomer only makes a difference where there is room for it.* The preview shows the room at cell scale; the hero shows a scientist comparing a crowded plate with a sparse one. Same thought, two scales.

**The mechanism in one sentence:** In a gut with many kinds of bacteria every niche — the fibre, the mucus, the bile acids — is already taken, so an outside signal like dead *L. plantarum* cell walls finds no open seat; in a gut with few kinds there is empty room, and that is the only group in which the trial saw weight, BMI and leptin fall.

**Priority:** news — this week (article published 2026-09-02).

**Truth note for the preview:** the cells are dead. They do not swim, divide or colonise. They *settle*. If the frame reads as a living culture taking over, it is telling the wrong story.

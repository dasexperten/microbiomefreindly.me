## The mechanism in one sentence, for Lisa's understanding

Flagellin — the protein bacteria build their swimming tails from — leaks out of a gut where flagellated bacteria have expanded, trips the TLR5 alarm receptor on neutrophils, which answer with IL-15; that pushes macrophages to make arachidonic acid, and organs far from the gut inflame. It is a candidate mechanism: the human side is description, the cut-the-chain test was done only in mice, and nothing in it is a treatment — so the pictures should feel like an observation, not a finding.

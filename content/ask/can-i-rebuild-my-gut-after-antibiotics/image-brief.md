**2. The mechanism in one clause (from the `answer` field)**

The gut community regrows close to what it was within about a month and a half, while nine species that were in every man before the course are still not found in most of them at day 180.

**3. The organism or structure, with its real morphology in plain words**

Not one species — a mixed community on a stretch of gut wall. Every shape below is named in the study behind the piece (Palleja et al., Nature Microbiology, 2018) or is that organism's ordinary textbook form. Nothing here is invented, and nothing here may be redrawn as something else:

- **Forked cells** — *Bifidobacterium*: short rods that split at one end into a Y, sometimes into a small branch. These are the ones the antibiotics thin out. Never spheres.
- **Long slender spindles** — *Fusobacterium nucleatum*: thin rods tapering to a point at both ends, longer than everything around them. One of the few that blooms in the emptied space.
- **Small round cells in pairs and short chains** — *Enterococcus faecalis*: ovoid cocci, two together or four to six in a row, never a long chain. Also a bloomer.
- **Plain straight rods with rounded ends** — the enterobacteria and the ordinary residents: some with fine hair-like tails, most without.
- **The ground they sit on** — a soft translucent mucus film lying over the gut lining, and beneath it the lining rising in a rank of finger-shaped folds. The community sits in the film, not on a bare surface.

No spores swelling a cell end, no spirals, no filaments, no biofilm sheet: none of those belong in this frame.

**4. The three-beat process — first, next, outcome**

1. **First:** a dense, varied field on the wall — forks, spindles, pairs, rods, all crowded together — is stripped in four days. The field thins to almost nothing; the few shapes still standing are the spindles and the small paired cocci, which take the emptied room.
2. **Next:** over roughly six weeks the field refills. Shape after shape comes back, the crowding returns, and by eye it reads as it did before.
3. **Outcome:** the refilled field is full and not the same — nine places in it stay empty. That is where the answer lives: recovery is real, and it is incomplete.

The eye must be able to walk that path in one pass: full → stripped → refilled with gaps.

**5. The single sourced number, shown by scale or quantity**

**Nine.** Nine species that were present in all 12 men before the course were still undetectable in most of them 180 days later — Palleja A. et al., *Nature Microbiology*, 2018 (`s1` in the article, doi 10.1038/s41564-018-0257-9, PMID 30349083).

Shown as **nine visibly empty places** in the refilled field — nine clear pockets in the mucus where a cell plainly used to stand, countable without effort, in an otherwise crowded frame. Nine, exactly: not eight, not a suggestion of "some". The number is never printed, never labelled, never written anywhere in the pixels. Quantity is the whole of it.

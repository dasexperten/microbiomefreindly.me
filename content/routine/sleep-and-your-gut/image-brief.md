**2. The mechanism in one clause (from the `answer` field)**

Sleep and the gut move together — ragged, irregular sleep travels with a community that holds fewer kinds of bacteria — but the two experiments that actually cut people's sleep disagree about whether the community moves at all.

The frame illustrates a **correlation**, not a demolition. Nothing in it may read as damage, disease or destruction. The wrong picture here is a wrecked gut; the right one is a gut that has lost variety while staying full.

**3. The organism or structure, with its real morphology in plain words**

Not one species — the mixed community that lives on a stretch of gut wall. Every shape below is either named in the article or is that group's ordinary textbook form. Nothing here is invented, and nothing here may be redrawn as something else:

- **Plain straight rods with rounded ends, single or in pairs** — *Faecalibacterium prausnitzii*, the butyrate producer the article names, and the *Bacteroides* rods alongside it. Non-motile, no spores, no tails. These are the workhorses of the frame and the most numerous shape in it.
- **Small round cells in pairs and short chains** — the coccal members of the Firmicutes side. Ovoid, two together or four to six in a row, never a long whip of a chain.
- **Slender rods, noticeably longer and thinner than the plain ones** — the ordinary long-rod residents that read as a different kind at a glance.
- **Forked cells** — short rods splitting at one end into a Y. Included only as one of the kinds that variety is counted in; they are not the subject here and must never be drawn as spheres.
- **The ground they sit on** — a soft translucent mucus film lying over the gut lining, and beneath it the lining rising in a rank of finger-shaped folds. The community sits in the film, not on a bare surface.

No spores swelling a cell end, no spirals, no filaments, no biofilm sheet, no fungal or yeast forms: none of those belong in this frame.

The real subject is **how many different shapes there are**, not how crowded the field is. Diversity in this piece means *kinds*, and the picture has to make kinds countable by eye.

**4. The three-beat process — first, next, outcome**

The eye reads one continuous stretch of gut wall from one side to the other, the community changing along it. One world, one field — not three plates side by side.

1. **First:** a calm, even daily rhythm. The community is arranged in a regular wave along the wall — evenly spaced crests and troughs — and it holds many distinct kinds at once: plain rods, paired cocci, slender long rods, forks, all clearly different from each other.
2. **Next:** the rhythm goes ragged. Along the wall the wave loses its regular spacing — crests bunch up, troughs stretch wide, the beat becomes uneven. This is the irregular sleep: the timing breaks before anything else does.
3. **Outcome:** the field stays full, and holds fewer kinds. Where four or five plainly different shapes stood, two or three now repeat. Nothing is emptied, nothing is destroyed, nothing is inflamed — the crowd is the same size and less varied, and the wave through it is out of step.

Full and varied on a steady beat → the beat goes ragged → still full, fewer kinds. That is the whole path, and it must be walkable in one pass.

**5. The single sourced number, shown by scale or quantity**

**None shown — clause dropped.** This piece carries no figure in `keyFacts` that can honestly be shown, and the clause is dropped rather than filled.

The number clause of the prompt paragraph is removed for this frame. That is a decision, not an omission, and here is the working so nobody re-opens it:

- The figures the article actually carries are counts of **people** — 720 adults, 26 men, 9 men, 11 adults, 30 older adults, 180 participants — and this lane forbids people, faces, hands and bodies in the frame. A participant count cannot be shown by scale or quantity without breaking that rule.
- The statistical figures — P = 0.0286, P = 0.0422, P = 0.0320, p = 0.013 — are not quantities of anything visible. They cannot be drawn at all.
- The review's 9 of 12 primary sleep outcomes favouring supplementation (`s8`) is a count of trial outcomes, not of anything that lives on the gut wall; drawing it would mean a tally or a chart.
- The one genuinely proportional finding, the shifted Firmicutes-to-Bacteroidetes ratio in the nine-man experiment, has **no numeric ratio in our sourced facts**. Drawing a proportion we did not source would be inventing the number, which is forbidden outright.

So: no number is expressed in this image, and none may be added at generation time. If a figure appears in a returned frame in any form — as a printed value, as a counted set standing in for a statistic, as a chart-like proportion — the frame is rejected.

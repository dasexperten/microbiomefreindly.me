- Beneath the lining: loose, soft tissue threaded with sparse fine fibres.
- Bacteria: the hub names no species for this story, so they are generic — short rods with rounded ends and small round cocci.
- Regulatory T cells (second frame): small round cells, much smaller than the macrophage, mostly filled by one large round nucleus, the surface lightly textured with very short projections.
- Short-chain fatty acids (second frame): tiny molecules, each a small cluster of a few linked beads.

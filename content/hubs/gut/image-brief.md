## The brief

**1. Slug (ASCII, language-neutral):** `gut`
File names: `gut-preview` · `gut-hero`.

**2. Article title (EN) + one-line summary:** "The gut microbiome: what the science says and what it can't" — the community of microbes in the large intestine digests fibre we cannot, makes short-chain fatty acids and vitamins, trains immunity and feeds the gut lining; it changes within days, and most of what it means for one person is not yet known.

**3. Organism / structure in the preview:** the community itself at work — a mixed crowd of colon bacteria on a fragment of plant fibre, the food the hub says our own enzymes cannot break down.

**Card (person, Owner 2026-09-16):** Lota from `refs/characters/` · a woman in her early thirties, a bus driver · the front steps of her own house, early evening.

**4. Morphology, in words (truth, Magnus):**
- Plant fibre: a torn sheet of plant cell walls — empty, many-sided cells joined like a honeycomb, thin ribbed walls; one edge frayed where it is being broken down.
- The bacteria: a mixed community, as the hub describes it, not one species. Short and longer rods with rounded ends, some slightly curved; small round cocci, single and in pairs. The hub also names archaea, fungi and viruses; they are left out so the frame stays readable — nothing drawn lies outside the hub.
- The gut wall (second frame): the lining of the colon — a flat surface of tall column-shaped cells dotted with the round openings of narrow pits. The colon has no finger-shaped villi, so none are drawn.
- Short-chain fatty acids such as butyrate: tiny molecules, each a short zigzag of a few linked beads.

**5. Preview mood (one line, warm science):** a honeycomb sheet of plant fibre adrift in the moist dim contents of the colon, a busy but calm crowd of rods and cocci feeding along its frayed edge, lit by bright clean morning light from the side; the frayed edge carries the only gold.

**Card scene (one line):** coming up her own front steps in the early evening with a cloth bag of vegetables on her hip, stopping to shift the weight — the widest section of the portal in the most ordinary moment it has: food comes home, and the community changes with it.

**Wardrobe and place:** plain 2026 clothing, no logos: a plain dark red jacket over jeans, trainers, hair pushed back; the front steps of a terraced house at 19:00, low golden evening sun straight down the street from behind her right shoulder, warm, with long shadows on the render.

**EMPTY FIELD (where the words will sit):** the left 45 % of the frame is the smooth rendered front wall of the house beside the steps — one even, evenly shaded plane. Nothing crosses it: no door, no window, no house number, no bell, no downpipe, no cable, no pattern, and no branch shadow with a readable shape. It lies inside the middle horizontal band (the top and bottom 11 % are air). She stands on the right third, head and shoulders inside that same band.

**6. Hero mechanism (the answer, one clause):** bacteria in the colon ferment the fibre we cannot digest into short-chain fatty acids that feed the cells of the gut wall.

**7. Three beats (first → next → outcome):** (1) fibre that escaped digestion arrives: torn honeycomb sheets of plant cell walls; (2) a mixed crowd of rods and cocci settles on the fibre and ferments it, breaking it into smaller and smaller pieces; (3) outcome: small short-chain fatty acid molecules drift from the crowd down into the column-shaped cells of the gut wall and are taken up.
